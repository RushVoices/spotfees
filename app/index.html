How it works (01)

One coin. One fee stream. Ten owners.

When a coin launches through Stacks, its pump.fun creator fees are wired to ten on-chain vaults at 10% each. Pump locks that split forever. Then the ten Stacks go up for auction.

01

Launch your coin

Create a coin on pump.fun from this page. One wallet prompt creates it, turns on fee sharing, and sends the creator fees to ten Stack vaults, 10% each. Pump locks the split.

02

Ten Stacks, one auction

Each Stack opens at a start price that drops every 15 seconds down to a floor. Take one when the price feels right. After every take the price restarts for the next Stack.

03

Earn as it trades

Every buy and sell of the coin pays creator fees. They land in the ten vaults in equal parts. Claim your share in SOL whenever you like, or send your Stack to someone else.

The auction (02)

The price falls until someone takes it.

A descending-price auction: no bidding wars, no gas races. You sign a maximum price; if someone beats you to it, your transaction fails and you pay nothing.

Launch (04)

Launch a coin. Sell its future.

Your coin goes live on pump.fun with its creator fees split into ten Stacks. You keep the auction proceeds, minus a 2% protocol fee, and can pre-buy up to five Stacks for yourself.
