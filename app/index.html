    How Slices work.

    What a Slice is, how the auction prices it, what happens when you take one, and how fees reach you.

    On this page Overview What a Slice is The auction Taking a Slice Earning fees Launching a token Terms FAQ

    Overview

    Every token earns creator fees as it trades. Slices splits that stream into ten equal shares and sells them in a price-falling auction. If you hold a Slice, you earn one tenth of the creator fees for as long as the token trades.

    The creator keeps the choice of how many Slices to hold from day one. Everyone else buys the rest at a price that starts high and steps down until someone takes it.

    What a Slice is

    A Slice is a numbered claim on a share of one token’s creator fees. Every launch has exactly ten, numbered 01 to 10, and each is worth 10% of the same stream.

    01020304050607080910

    Example: a creator who pre-bought three Slices holds 30% of the fee stream. Seven Slices go to auction.

    StateMeaning
    CreatorPre-bought by the token’s creator at the floor price, before the auction opened.
    OpenUp for auction at the current price.
    YoursHeld by your wallet. Fees for this Slice are credited to you.

    The auction

    Open Slices are sold at a price that falls on a fixed schedule. Every 15 seconds the price drops by one step, until it reaches the floor. It stays at the floor until the Slice is taken.

    price = max(floor, start − drop × ⌊seconds ÷ 15⌋)

    For example, in Degen mode after 45 seconds the price is 1.5 − 0.125 × 3 = 1.125 SOL.

    ModeStartFloorDrop / 15sTime to floorBuilt for
    Degen1.5 SOL0.5 SOL0.125 SOL2 minCheap Slices, fast price discovery
    Builder4 SOL1 SOL0.2 SOL3 min 45 sPremium Slices for launches with real backing

    The creator picks the mode when launching. You can watch both schedules play out on the chart on the home page.

    Taking a Slice

    Taking a Slice is one transaction that you sign in your wallet. It either completes in full or does not happen at all.

    1. Choose an open Slice

      Pick a Slice that is open and check the price shown. The price keeps falling while you decide.

    2. Sign once

      Your wallet shows the Slice number and the most you are willing to pay. That limit is the price you saw when you clicked.

    3. The price is calculated at the moment of purchase

      The program works out the price from the auction’s start, floor, drop and elapsed time. It does not rely on what your screen displayed. If the result is above your limit, the transaction fails and you pay nothing.

    4. You pay and the Slice is assigned to you

      The price is taken from your wallet in SOL and the Slice is recorded against your address. If someone else took the same Slice a moment earlier, your transaction fails and your SOL stays put.

    5. You start earning

      From the next fees the token earns, one tenth is credited to you for this Slice.

    Earning fees

    Taking a Slice does not pay you anything by itself. You earn from trading activity that happens afterwards.

    Every launch has ten vaults, one per Slice, that only the Slices program can pay out from. The auction does not open until the program has checked on-chain that the token’s creator fees are wired to those vaults and can’t be changed.

    On Pump, the coin’s fee sharing is set to the ten vaults at 10% each. Pump locks that list permanently, so not even the creator can change it. On Meteora, the pool’s creator role is handed to the Slices program, which can only send the fees to the ten vaults.

    To get paid, press Collect fees: it pays everything the token has earned so far into all ten vaults at once, and anyone can do it. Then Claim sends your vault’s balance to your wallet in SOL. Fees earned by a Slice before anyone buys it stay in its vault and go to whoever takes it.

    More trading volume means more fees. A quiet token earns little, and a Slice has no guaranteed return.

    Launching a token

    Creators set the terms on the launch form and sign once.

    SettingWhat it does
    Trading venueWhere the token trades: Pump (pump.fun) or Meteora.
    TokenName (up to 32 characters), symbol (up to 10) and an image of up to 5 MB.
    Auction modeDegen or Builder pricing, as in the table above.
    Pre-buy Slices0 to 5 Slices you keep before the public auction. Since the price would be paid to you, you only pay the 2% fee on the floor price.
    Dev buyOptional SOL spent on the token itself at launch, shown with an estimate of tokens received.

    The total you sign is 2% × pre-buy Slices × floor price + dev buy, plus about 0.04 SOL of network rent for the new accounts. For example, three Degen pre-buys and a 0.2 SOL dev buy come to 0.03 + 0.2 + 0.04 ≈ 0.27 SOL.

    Terms

    Unaudited software. The Slices program has not had a security audit yet. Use small amounts, and check the network badge on the launch form before you sign.

    TermRule
    Where Slice payments goTo the token’s creator, minus a 2% protocol fee

    Price after a Slice is takenRestarts at the start price
    Cut of the fee streamNone. Slices keep 100% of the creator fees
    Transferring a SliceSupported by the program; earnings move with it
    Slices that never sellPrice stays at the floor; fees wait in the vault

    One thing outside Slices’ control: Pump and Meteora can change their own programs and fee rules, including Pump’s admin tools for fee sharing.

    FAQ

    Can two people take the same Slice?

    No. Only the first transaction to land wins. The other fails in full and the SOL is not spent.

    What if the price drops after I click?

    You pay the price at the moment the transaction lands, never more than the limit you signed. If it is lower, you pay the lower price.

    What is the difference between Degen and Builder?

    Degen starts at 1.5 SOL and drops to a 0.5 SOL floor in two minutes. Builder starts at 4 SOL and drops to a 1 SOL floor in three minutes and forty-five seconds.

    Is a Slice guaranteed to earn money?

    No. Earnings depend on how much the token trades. A Slice can earn less than you paid for it.

    Do I have to press Collect fees?

    Fees build up on Pump or Meteora until someone collects them. Anyone can press Collect fees, and it pays all ten Slices at once. Then each holder claims their own share.
